Rewards: 25 / 50 / 75 / 100 credits unlock 20 / 30 / 50 / 100 coins.

Daily progress resets at local midnight. Cumulative chest progress survives.
Existing six-task claims are migrated while retaining earned credits and coins.

Presentation:
Full-screen portrait background covers the entire canvas including unsafe edges.
Interactive card fills the safe-area height. Tall displays expand the list rows;
shorter displays scroll. No title banner or information button.
Actual gameplay ability icons are reused for all four ability tasks.
All gifts can show the compact anchored coin/amount preview while locked or claimed.
Claimed gift lids open, with animated shine. The duck writes on collection.
Claimed task rows darken and move to the end of the task list.

Notification markers:
Home Daily Tasks: a completed task, unlocked milestone, or cumulative chest can be claimed.
Home Shop: daily shop gift available, or completed coin task awaiting its task claim.
Shop daily free-coin button: saved once-per-day shop gift available.
Home Lucky Spin: actual available spins > 0.
Markers are non-interactive and refresh from saved state, without intercepting input.
